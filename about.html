About Tech4Pride Programme

Programme Overview

The Tech4Pride programme is designed to equip young Queer people with pratical digital skills needed to succeed in the modern tech industry.

It focuses on hands-on learning, mentorship, and real-world projects that helps students grow from begineers into job-ready developers.

Why Frontend Development

  • It helps you build visible and interactive websites
  • High demand for frontend developers globally
  • Great entry point into the tech industry
  • Allows creativity and problem-solving
  • You can start building projects quickly

Student Testimonial

"Before joining Tech4Pride, I had little direction in tech. Now, I am more confident and learning to build web pages from scratch and understand how the internet works. The learning experience has been both challenging and empowering."
